Methodology

TrendRadar monitors public web and search signals to help creators and marketers investigate emerging opportunities.

Sources

We use public Google Trends and GDELT signals. Some additional opportunities are found through grounded web research, with source links shown when available.

Momentum

Velocity compares recent observations with earlier observations. It is a directional indicator, not a verified audience total or prediction.

Verify before acting

Every signal should be checked at its original source before action.
